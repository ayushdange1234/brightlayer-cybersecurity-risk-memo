# BrightLayer Stores – Cybersecurity Risk Memo

## 1. FIVE IMPORTANT ASSETS

1. **Employee Email Accounts** – Used for business communication, customer interactions, password resets, and sharing sensitive information.
2. **Shared Computers** – Used by employees to access email, cloud storage, and other business systems. They are essential for daily operations.
3. **Company Website** – Provides the public-facing presence of BrightLayer Stores. A compromise could affect the company's reputation and customer trust.
4. **Cloud Storage with Invoices** – Contains important financial and business records. Unauthorized access or modification could cause financial loss.
5. **User Passwords** – Protect email, cloud storage, and other company resources. Weak or reused passwords can allow attackers to gain unauthorized access.

## 2. THREE POTENTIAL THREATS

1. **Phishing Attack** – A cybercriminal sends a fraudulent email to trick an employee into revealing credentials or opening a malicious attachment.
2. **Unauthorized Network Access** – An attacker attempts to gain access through the public Wi-Fi router or intercept network traffic.
3. **Malware/Ransomware** – Malicious software infects a shared computer and may steal, damage, or encrypt company data.

## 3. THREE VULNERABILITIES

1. **Weak or Reused Passwords** – Employees may use simple or repeated passwords across multiple business accounts.
2. **Poorly Secured Wi-Fi Router** – Weak administrator credentials, outdated firmware, or inadequate wireless security could allow unauthorized access.
3. **Insufficient Endpoint Protection** – Shared computers may lack updated antivirus/EDR, security patches, and restrictions on software installation.

## 4. ASSOCIATED RISKS

* **Phishing + Weak/Reused Passwords:** Stolen credentials could allow attackers to access employee email and cloud storage, exposing invoices and confidential business information.
* **Unauthorized Network Access + Poorly Secured Wi-Fi:** An attacker could gain network access, intercept traffic, or attempt to access connected systems, resulting in data exposure or business disruption.
* **Malware/Ransomware + Insufficient Endpoint Protection:** Malware could encrypt or damage business files, causing downtime, recovery costs, and loss of productivity.

## 5. SECURITY RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Enable MFA** for employee email and cloud storage to reduce the impact of stolen passwords.
2. **Enforce strong, unique passwords** and encourage the use of a password manager.
3. **Secure the Wi-Fi router** by changing default credentials, using WPA2/WPA3, updating firmware, and disabling unnecessary services.
4. **Deploy endpoint protection/EDR** and ensure operating systems and applications receive regular security updates.
5. **Apply least-privilege access** so employees cannot install unauthorized software or make unnecessary system changes.
6. **Provide phishing awareness training** so employees can identify suspicious emails, links, and attachments.
7. **Maintain regular backups** of important business data, including invoices, and periodically test restoration.

Overall, BrightLayer Stores should prioritize protecting employee accounts, securing the network, and improving endpoint security because these controls directly reduce the risk of unauthorized access, data loss, and operational disruption.
